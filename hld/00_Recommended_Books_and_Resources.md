# Recommended Books & Resources: High-Level System Design (HLD)

A curated list of authoritative books, engineering blogs, case studies, and reference architectures for mastering High-Level System Design.

---

## 1. Essential Books

1. **System Design Interview – An Insider's Guide (Volume 1 & Volume 2)**  
   *Author:* Alex Xu, Sugu Sougoumarane  
   *Focus:* The canonical preparation books for system design interviews, covering 25+ real-world case studies (Rate Limiter, Key-Value Store, Distributed Search, Payment System, Stock Exchange).

2. **Designing Data-Intensive Applications**  
   *Author:* Martin Kleppmann  
   *Focus:* Deep technical foundations of reliable, scalable, and maintainable systems. Essential reading for understanding data models, replication, partitioning, and consensus.

3. **Software Engineering at Google: Lessons Learned from Programming Over Time**  
   *Authors:* Titus Winters, Tom Manshreck, Hyrum Wright  
   *Focus:* Engineering culture, design reviews, technical debt management, and large-scale infrastructure operations at Google.

## 2. Engineering Blogs & Architecture Resources

* **[ByteByteGo System Design Newsletter](https://bytebytego.com/)** — Visual breakdown of distributed system architectures, microservices, and database internals.
* **[High Scalability Blog](http://highscalability.com/)** — Real-world architecture breakdowns of Netflix, Uber, Twitter, YouTube, and WhatsApp.
* **[Uber Engineering Blog](https://www.uber.com/blog/engineering/)** — Geospatial indexing (H3), real-time dispatch, and distributed tracing.
* **[Netflix TechBlog](https://netflixtechblog.com/)** — Microservice architecture, Chaos Engineering (Chaos Monkey), video encoding pipelines, and global CDN delivery (Open Connect).
* **[AWS Architecture Center](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/)** — Reference architectures, Well-Architected Framework, and cloud infrastructure designs.
